## 🌈 Where Color Remains

**Functional colors kept for clarity**:
- 🟢 **Green**: Low risk zones (vegetation < 1.0m)
- 🟡 **Orange**: Moderate risk zones (1.0-2.5m)
- 🔴 **Red**: High risk zones (> 2.5m) and alerts

These are **functional colors** that indicate risk levels, not decorative!
